Grounding

A calm guide for moments of anxiety, panic, or overwhelm.

Come back to the present

Grounding walks you through simple sensory exercises designed to help redirect your attention toward what is happening around you right now. The interface is intentionally quiet, clear, and easy to follow when thinking feels difficult.

Guided grounding

Step through familiar grounding prompts one at a time, without clutter or pressure.

Paced breathing

Use a gentle breathing timer to slow down and settle into a steadier rhythm.

Simple by design

No account, social feed, or complicated setup. Open the app and begin.
