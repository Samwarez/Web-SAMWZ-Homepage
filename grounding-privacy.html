Grounding

Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026

Grounding is designed to be a simple, private tool for guided grounding and breathing exercises. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information collected

The current version of Grounding does not require an account and does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

App data

Grounding's exercises and settings operate locally on your device. The app does not upload your exercise activity or responses to a server.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version of Grounding does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Permissions

Grounding does not request access to sensitive device features such as your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files for its core grounding and breathing features.

Children's privacy

Grounding does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not collect personal information, no age-specific user profile is created.

Changes to this policy

If Grounding's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page will also be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Samwz.com contact page.
